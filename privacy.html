Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to AdOneClick ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our unified ad-to-lead CRM and marketing automation platform. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining enterprise-grade security for all incoming ad lead payloads.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as lead payload data streamed through your connected advertising platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads) and CRM destinations. This includes:

  • Account credentials, name, work email address, company details, and billing records
  • Lead form responses and survey answers submitted by prospects on your connected ad campaigns
  • Channel authentication tokens (OAuth) for ad account sync
  • Telemetry, API response codes, and ingestion latency logs to ensure platform uptime

3. How We Use Information

We process your data strictly to deliver, optimize, and protect our integration services:

  • To provide real-time native ad ingestion, visual field mapping, and routing to your CRM
  • To trigger automated speed-to-lead SLA timers and team notifications
  • To compute closed-loop CAC, ad spend attribution, and sales conversion metrics
  • To enforce strict data security standards and comply with applicable statutory laws

4. Google API Data Usage

When you connect your Google Ads account, we access Google API data solely for the purpose of integrating with your Google Ads campaigns and lead form assets. We only access scopes explicitly authorized by you. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Meta & Facebook Ads Data Usage

When you connect your Meta / Facebook Ads account, we access lead generation form assets exclusively to stream incoming lead responses directly to your configured CRM pipeline. Our use of Meta data complies with all terms of the Meta Platform Terms & Developer Policies.

6. Data Retention & Zero-Drop Security

Lead payloads are transferred immediately upon receipt. In the event of downstream endpoint unavailability, payloads are stored temporarily in encrypted transit buffers with automated retry schedules. Data is retained only as long as necessary to complete delivery or as required by your subscription settings.

7. Data Security Standards

We implement state-of-the-art technical and organizational security controls, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 bit encryption at rest, isolated tenant schemas, and continuous vulnerability scanning.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

In accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, you have the right to request access, rectification, portability, or complete permanent deletion of all personal data held within your workspace. To submit a data deletion request, email us at rirtshivchandra@gmail.com. We fulfill verified requests within 30 days.
