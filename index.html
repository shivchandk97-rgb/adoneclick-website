Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about connecting Meta Ads, upcoming channels, custom fields, or team onboarding? We've got answers.

Which ad platforms are available right now for lead management?
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Lead Generation Forms) is fully available right now with instant bi-directional webhook synchronization. Custom Inbound Webhooks & REST API are also live. Integrations for Google Ads Lead Form Assets, TikTok Ads, and LinkedIn Ads are actively in development and rolling out soon.
How do dynamic custom fields work with Meta ad forms?
You can create unlimited custom fields in AdOneClick (Text, Currency, Numbers, Single/Multi-Select Dropdowns, and Dates). When mapping your Meta Instant Form questions, each answer field attaches seamlessly to the lead record and can be used to trigger automation conditions or filter leads in the Kanban pipeline.
How fast does lead management setup take?
Setup takes less than 5 minutes. Simply log into AdOneClick, connect your Meta ad account via OAuth, select the ad forms you wish to sync, and your leads begin flowing into your Kanban pipeline in real-time.
How does the Speed-to-Lead SLA countdown timer work?
You define your target response window (e.g. 5 minutes, 15 minutes, or 1 hour). When a lead arrives from a Meta ad, an SLA timer begins counting down in real-time. If unaddressed, the badge shifts from green to yellow to flashing red, sending escalation alerts to sales managers before the prospect turns cold.
How do automation workflows and team routing work?
With our visual drag-and-drop workflow canvas, you can automatically distribute incoming leads to online sales representatives using weighted Round-Robin assignment, send instant personalized WhatsApp/SMS alerts to prospects, and trigger automated reminders.
Can I invite my sales team members and manage their roles?
Yes! AdOneClick includes full team collaboration with Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Sales Manager, and Sales Representative) so each team member has the exact visibility and permissions they need.
